Title: The Aurora Research Station

The Aurora Research Station is a fictional scientific facility located on Northstar Island.

The station was established in 1984 by a team of 17 researchers.

Its main laboratory is located 12 meters underground to protect sensitive instruments from extreme weather.

The station contains three observation towers.

The tallest observation tower is 31 meters high.

In 1998, researchers installed a specialized telescope called Aurora-7.

Aurora-7 was designed primarily to observe distant variable stars.

The station's communication antenna is 46 meters tall.

In 2006, the station received a new solar-power system containing 240 solar panels.

The station can store enough energy to operate independently for approximately 18 days.

SPECIAL TEST FACT:

The original research team gave the station the internal identification code AR-5816.

The code is used for testing the knowledge retrieval system.

END OF DOCUMENT.
