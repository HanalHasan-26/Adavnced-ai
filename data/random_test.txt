Title: The Blue Harbor Lighthouse

The Blue Harbor Lighthouse was built in 1897 on a small island called Bellstone Island.

The lighthouse tower is 42 meters tall and has 186 stone steps leading to the observation room.

Its original light used an oil-powered mechanism. In 1956, the lighthouse was converted to an electric lighting system.

The lighthouse keeper's house was located 75 meters east of the main tower.

In 1978, a small weather station was installed near the northern wall of the lighthouse.

The lighthouse was automated in 1992, and no permanent lighthouse keeper has lived there since then.

The observation room contains an old brass telescope manufactured in France.

SPECIAL TEST FACT:

The lighthouse's emergency radio identification number is 6384.
